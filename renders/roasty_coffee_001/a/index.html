Sourcing

Sourced at altitude, flown without a layover

Roasty buys green coffee from eleven countries, but two do most of the talking. In Cuba we work the Sierra Maestra and the Turquino pass, where old bourbon and typica grow slowly in thin mountain air. In Argentina we are early to a young growing region — Salta and Jujuy, terraces between 1,600 and 1,900 metres that were planting vines a decade ago.

Every lot travels under one manifest: bought at the farm gate, palletised, and in the roastery within fourteen days of leaving the mill. We publish what we paid, what we shipped, and the day it was roasted on the back of every bag.
